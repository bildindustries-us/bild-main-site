The daily rhythm.

Early Morning
Sunrise · Breath Work · Envisioning

The day begins before it gets loud. Sunrise on the terrace. Intentional breathing. Five minutes of specific vision for the day ahead. This is the practice the retreat teaches — and every morning it gets easier and more powerful.

Morning & Afternoon
3–4 Deep Sessions Daily

Led by Jeff or by world-class guest coaches with decades of experience working with high-level men. Each session goes into a specific area of the BILD Six Pillars. No surface conversations. No polished performances. Just honest work.

Between Sessions
Space to Process

Private journaling time. Group walks. Ocean swims. The side-by-side ride down the coast to a local restaurant where the conversations that need to happen in small groups happen naturally. Space is not empty time — it is where the work lands.

Meals
Catered · Around the Table

Most meals are catered at the villa. Breakfast is easy and relaxed. Lunch and dinner are moments of real connection — food, laughter, honest conversation. One lunch is the coastal ride. One dinner is the night you will not forget.

Evenings
Under the Stars

No phones. No distraction. The group sits on the beach, lies in the sand, watches the stars above the Sea of Cortez. These are the hours where something releases that could not release in a session. Men talk to each other like they have not talked in years.

Throughout
Private Time · Your Own Pace

This is not a schedule you survive. There is space to nap, walk alone, sit with what surfaced in a session. The retreat is designed for men to connect in the way that suits them best — not to force a mode that doesn't fit.

Why This Is Different

Not a conference.
Not a workshop.
An encounter.

World-Class Guest Coaches

Every retreat brings in elite coaches who have spent decades working at the intersection of faith, psychology, trauma, and high performance. These are not speakers — they are practitioners who guide sessions based on their specific expertise in ways you won't find anywhere else.

The Environment Does the Work

Los Barriles is not a conference center. It is a private villa on the hills above the Sea of Cortez. The desert, the ocean, the silence, the sky at night — the environment itself creates conditions for depth that are impossible to manufacture in a hotel ballroom.

Selected Brotherhood

Not every man who applies attends. The group is curated so that every man in the room is there for the same reason: to do real work, not to observe it. The energy of the room is the most powerful thing about the retreat. A room of rare men doing deep work is unlike anything most men have experienced.

The Full Arc — All Six Pillars

The retreat doesn't cover one topic. It works the full BILD arc — Powerlessness, Accountability, Co-Creation, Purpose, Forgiveness, and Imagination — through the lens of each man's specific life and specific moment. You don't get a general teaching. You get the teaching applied to you.

The Care Package

Before you arrive, a branded BILD care package ships to your door. Everything you need to show up ready — including the gear that marks you as part of this brotherhood. The retreat begins before you land.

Post-Retreat Follow-Up

The retreat doesn't end when you leave. Jeff personally follows up with every man 30 days after. The work that surfaces in Los Barriles needs time and support to land. That follow-up is built in.
