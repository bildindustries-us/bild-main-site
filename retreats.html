What This Is

The Los Barriles experience —
closer to home.

The BILD Day Retreats were designed for the man who knows he needs this work but can't commit to four days in Mexico right now. Same depth. Same honesty. Same brotherhood. Compressed into a Thursday-Saturday format that busy men can actually protect on their calendars.

These are not conferences. There are no speakers presenting from a stage. There is Jeff Boyer, a small group of serious men, the Six Pillar curriculum, and two full days to go further than most men go in a year of self-improvement content.

The Schedule

What the three days
actually look like.

Every hour is intentional. There is deep work and there is space. There is group intensity and private reflection. The format is designed so that men who can only give 2.5 days still walk away with real shifts — not just inspiration.

THU
Jun 26
Day One — Thursday
Arrive. Set everything down.
3:00 PM
Arrive and check in to hotel. Get settled. No agenda yet.
6:30 PM
Welcome Dinner — the first meal together. Men introduce themselves with one honest answer to one honest question. The tone is set here.
8:00 PM
Opening Session — 90 minutes — Jeff opens the retreat. Why you're here. What the next two days will ask of you. The one rule: no hiding.
9:30 PM
End of the evening. Time to process. Get to bed — tomorrow starts early.
FRI
Jun 27
Day Two — Friday
Go deep. Face yourself.
6:30 AM
Sunrise + Breathwork + Envisioning — optional but powerful. Five minutes of specific imagination before the day begins. The men who show up for this leave different.
8:00 AM
Breakfast together
9:00 AM
Session 1 — Powerlessness + Accountability — The False Power Move Cycle. Naming what you actually reach for. The private vs. public gap and what it costs everyone around you.
11:00 AM
Breakout groups + journaling — small groups of 3 men working through the morning teaching together
12:30 PM
Lunch
2:00 PM
Session 2 — Purpose + Forgiveness — The funeral question. The weight you've been carrying. The ceiling above every other area of your life you don't know is there.
4:00 PM
Group Challenge — something physical and collaborative in the Temecula hills. Gets men out of their heads. Brotherhood builds fastest here.
7:00 PM
Dinner + open conversation — no agenda. The best conversations happen here.
9:00 PM
Bonfire / stargazing — men sit together under the sky. No phones. No performance.
SAT
Jun 28
Day Three — Saturday
Go home. As a different man.
7:00 AM
Optional morning walk or quiet reflection time
8:00 AM
Breakfast — last meal together
9:00 AM
Closing Session — Create With Him + Imagination — The vision you're building toward. What you now see that you couldn't see 48 hours ago. The commitment you make before you leave.
11:00 AM
Brotherhood Send-Off — every man speaks one sentence. What shifted. The room holds it.
12:00 PM
Lunch and checkout. Fly or drive home.
